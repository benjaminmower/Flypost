💡 How to Post an Open House Click to expand

Include What (property address), When (date & times), and a link to the listing — Flypost will handle the rest.

Required Information

🏠
Property Address Full street address, city, and zip code
📅
Date & Time Day, date, start time, and end time

Optional but highly recommended!

• Listing price
• Beds & baths
• Link to Zillow, Realtor.com, or your listing page
• Agent name and contact info
• Special features or highlights

Examples

✅ Minimum Required
Open house Sunday, Feb 1st from 1:00 PM to 4:00 PM at 2682 34th St, Santa Monica, CA 90403.
⭐ Better (with details)
Open house Sunday, Feb 1st from 1:00 PM to 4:00 PM at 2682 34th St, Santa Monica, CA 90403. Beautiful 3 bed, 2 bath home listed at $1.5M. Hosted by Jane Smith, Coldwell Banker. https://www.zillow.com/homedetails/2682-34th-St/...
❌ Won't Work
"Open house this Sunday" ← missing address, date & times
"Open house at 34th St" ← missing full address, date & times
"Open house 1pm" ← missing address, date & end time
