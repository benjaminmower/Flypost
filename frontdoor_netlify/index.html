Protocol FAQ

How do I "capture" truth at my event?

You Deploy Flypost. By minting your open house into the Registry, you create a geofenced capture window. Visitors check in to convert physical proximity into an immutable data point.

What happens if an event has zero check-ins?

The Registry records a "Zero-Signal Event." In a post-truth market, a verified record of zero attendance is a powerful tool for price adjustments—it is ground truth, not guesswork.
