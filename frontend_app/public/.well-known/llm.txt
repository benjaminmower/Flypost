# Flypost App

Flypost App is the installable consumer PWA for discovering nearby flyers.

Primary URL: https://app.goflypost.com
API base: https://api.goflypost.com

Capabilities:
- Swipe nearby flyers from GET /v1/events/near
- Save flyers locally in the browser
- Publish authenticated flyer posts through POST /api/parse-and-publish
